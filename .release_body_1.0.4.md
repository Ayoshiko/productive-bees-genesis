## 概述

资源蜜蜂：创世 `1.0.4` 面向 Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge。本次更新加入 AE2 拉取库存保留功能，并继续改善大量机器与高倍加速下的运行表现。

## 更新内容

### 新增

- **AE2 拉取库存模式**：每个过滤条目都可以设置网络保留量。库存模式、每次拉取数量和无限拉取彼此独立，即使开启无限拉取，也不会取走设置为保留的库存。
- **更完整的拉取配置界面**：每个条目会显示对应物品在 AE2 网络中的当前库存；右键可切换库存模式，Shift+右键可设置保留量，齿轮菜单可一键设置全部条目。
- **大容量保留与配置持久化**：保留量不再受 16384 上限限制，并会随世界存档、机器挖掘或扳手拆卸、工厂安装器升级和配置卡复制完整保留。

### 变更

- **无限拉取按条目生效**：单个条目的无限拉取不再影响其它蜜脾；Shift 点击仍可快速恢复原有的全局无限拉取模式，并继续遵守各条目的库存保留量。
- **AE2 供能更直接**：机器会按当前能量升级后的真实容量补满缓存，空闲机器也可以正常充满；能量充足时，能量条不再长期停留在较低水位。

### 修复

- **高倍加速下的保留量准确性**：同一游戏刻内的实际提取会立即计入库存，JDTE 等高倍 tick 加速不会让机器突破设置的保留量。
- **多条目拉取数量准确性**：开启某个条目的无限拉取不再扩大整台机器的拉取配额，其它条目仍严格使用各自设置的数量。
- **工厂状态一致性**：修复批量产物回送期间，外部槽位变化可能让其它处理通道保留旧状态的问题。
- **升级后容量即时更新**：安装或拆除 Mekanism 能量升级后，机器会立即按新容量重新计算，不再沿用旧缓存。

### 性能

- **减少 AE2 库存访问**：同一种物品每轮只从 ME 网络提取一次，再分配到机器输入槽；蜜脾种类扫描与库存查询会复用结果，减少大量机器持续运行时的重复访问。
- **慢网络保护**：当外部存储响应缓慢时，拉取与产物回送会使用共享预算和渐进退避，避免高倍加速反复压迫同一 AE2 网络；正常网络保持原有吞吐。
- **降低供能与产物回送开销**：AE2 供能减少重复模拟和配方需求扫描，批量产物写入使用增量状态更新，降低高并行工厂的额外计算。

## English

Productive Bees Genesis `1.0.4` targets Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge. This release adds AE2 pull-stock reserves and improves sustained performance with many machines or accelerated ticks.

### Added

- **AE2 pull-stock mode**: every filter entry can keep a configured amount in network storage. Stock mode, per-pull amount, and unlimited pull are independent, so unlimited pulling still respects the reserve.
- **A more complete pull configuration screen**: every entry shows the matching item's current AE2 network stock. Right click toggles stock mode, Shift+right click edits the reserve, and the gear menu can configure all entries at once.
- **Large reserves with full persistence**: reserve values are no longer limited to 16,384 and persist through world saves, mining or wrench removal, factory installers, and configuration-card copies.

### Changed

- **Unlimited pull is scoped per entry**: enabling unlimited pull for one entry no longer affects unrelated combs. Shift click still restores the legacy global-unlimited mode, while configured stock reserves remain enforced.
- **More direct AE2 charging**: machines refill against their real capacity after energy upgrades and can fill while idle, so a well-powered machine no longer sits at a low buffer level.

### Fixed

- **Accurate reserves under tick acceleration**: committed extractions update network stock immediately within the same game tick, preventing JDTE and similar accelerators from pulling below a configured reserve.
- **Correct amounts across multiple entries**: unlimited pull on one entry no longer expands the whole machine's pull allowance; other entries continue to use their own configured amounts.
- **Consistent factory state**: fixed stale process state when external slot changes occur during batched product output.
- **Immediate capacity updates**: installing or removing Mekanism energy upgrades now recalculates machine capacity without retaining a stale cached value.

### Performance

- **Fewer AE2 inventory calls**: each item is extracted from ME storage once per round and then distributed locally. Comb discovery and stock queries reuse their results to reduce repeated work across many active machines.
- **Slow-network protection**: pull and product-output operations share a time budget and progressively back off when external storage responds slowly. Healthy networks retain their normal throughput.
- **Lower charging and output overhead**: AE2 charging avoids duplicate simulation and recipe-demand scans, while batched product output updates process state incrementally.
