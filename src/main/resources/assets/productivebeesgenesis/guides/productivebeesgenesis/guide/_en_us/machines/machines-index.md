# Core Machines

This mod has only two core machines: the Mechanical Apiary breeds bees and produces combs, while the Mekanism Centrifuge turns combs into resources. Understand them first — upgrades and automation only make sense afterwards.

The two machines are documented **separately**, not lumped together:

- [Mekanism Apiary](apiary.md): bee and cage slots, bee tooltips, output paging, the **Feeder window** (conversion toggle, per-slot disable, paging, stats panel), apiary-side PB upgrades, direct centrifuge links, and product routing.
- [Mekanism Centrifuge](centrifuge.md): input/output slots, fluid tank, the **input-return button**, the **multi-fluid tanks window**, smelting compatibility, direct output, the **AE2 input entry point**, and centrifuge-side PB upgrades.

What both machines share — energy, progress, redstone, security, side configuration, and auto-eject — is documented once in [Common Screens and Controls](../gui.md).
